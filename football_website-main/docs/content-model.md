# Proposed content model

## Club

- Name, short name, founding year, location
- History and background
- Mission, vision, values, objectives
- Contact and approved social links
- Management structure

## Team and season

- Team name and age category
- Season label
- League and competition
- Coaches and management
- Squad members
- Team-level performance summary

## Member profile

- Full and display name
- Slug / public profile URL
- Biography and background
- Player position or staff role
- Team/squad
- Shirt number
- Experience, skills, and qualifications
- Achievements and awards
- Season statistics and performance records
- Approved portrait, gallery, video, and social/contact links
- Draft/published status

## Match and statistics

- Date, opponent, venue, competition
- Home/away designation
- Score and result
- Starting lineup and substitutions
- Goals, assists, cards, clean sheets, and minutes
- Match report and approved media

## Achievement

- Title, year/date, competition
- Result or award
- Description and media
- Related team/member profiles

## News and announcement

- Title, slug, excerpt, body
- Category, author, feature image
- Draft, scheduled, or published state
- Publication date and related records

## Event or programme

- Name, description, date/time, venue
- Activity type and status
- Registration/contact details
- Related media and news

## Gallery item

- Image/video file and responsive variants
- Caption, alt text, credit, and capture date
- Album/category and related records
- Consent/approval state

## Partnership and enquiry

- Enquiry type, contact details, message
- Consent, status, assignment, and notes
- Sponsorship tier and approved benefits where applicable

## User and audit record

- Authenticated user identity
- Role and permissions
- Login/security metadata
- Content action, timestamp, and changed record
