Partnership levels

Choose your impact.

Choose a partnership level that fits your organisation. Commercial terms can be discussed and confirmed with the club.

Bronze

A starting partnership package for organisations that want to support Matsapha Roses FC and gain recognition within the club community.

  • Official Bronze Partner recognition
  • Logo inclusion on selected promotional materials
  • Social-media recognition
  • Sponsor listing on selected club materials
  • Sponsor acknowledgement at selected events
  • Partnership certificate
  • Recognition in the club's annual impact report
  • Opportunity to provide products or services in addition to financial support

Silver

Increased visibility and engagement through match-day, digital and promotional opportunities.

  • Enhanced logo placement
  • Regular social-media exposure
  • Match-day/event branding
  • Sponsor mentions in selected club communications
  • One branded activation opportunity
  • Limited player/team appearance opportunities
  • Inclusion in selected club media content
  • Hospitality opportunities
  • Partnership certificate
  • Recognition in season-end communications

Gold

A major partnership level designed for organisations seeking strong visibility and community engagement.

  • Official Major Partner status
  • Prominent logo placement on agreed club assets
  • Prominent match-day branding
  • Dedicated sponsor social-media campaign
  • Multiple sponsor activation opportunities
  • Community or youth-development activity
  • Player/team appearances for approved activities
  • Sponsor feature in selected club media content
  • Partnership certificate
  • Featured recognition in the annual impact report
  • Priority discussion regarding partnership renewal

Platinum

The highest partnership level, offering premium visibility, activation and community-impact opportunities.

  • Highest-level branding available within the agreed partnership
  • Premium logo placement on official club assets
  • Prominent match-day branding
  • Dedicated digital and social-media campaign
  • Multiple corporate activation opportunities
  • Player/team appearances for approved corporate activities
  • VIP hospitality opportunities
  • Joint public-relations and media opportunities
  • Naming rights for an agreed programme, tournament or community initiative
  • Event branding
  • Category exclusivity where commercially appropriate
  • Dedicated community-impact initiative
  • Featured position in the annual impact report
  • First-right discussion regarding renewal
